# Healthy Recipes Collection

## Overnight Oats
Overnight oats are a nutritious no-cook breakfast. Combine half a cup of rolled oats with half a cup of milk or yogurt in a jar. Add one tablespoon of chia seeds and a drizzle of honey. Mix well, cover, and refrigerate overnight for at least 6 hours. In the morning, top with fresh berries, sliced banana, and a sprinkle of nuts. This meal is rich in fiber, protein, and omega-3 fatty acids. You can prepare multiple jars on Sunday for the entire week.

## Greek Salad
Greek salad is a refreshing Mediterranean dish. Chop two large tomatoes, one cucumber, and half a red onion into bite-sized pieces. Add half a cup of Kalamata olives and 100 grams of crumbled feta cheese. For the dressing, whisk together three tablespoons of olive oil, one tablespoon of red wine vinegar, one teaspoon of dried oregano, salt, and pepper. Toss everything together and serve immediately. This salad is packed with vitamins, healthy fats, and antioxidants.

## Grilled Chicken with Vegetables
Marinate two chicken breasts in a mixture of lemon juice, garlic, olive oil, and herbs for at least 30 minutes. Chop bell peppers, zucchini, and broccoli into even pieces. Grill the chicken for 6-7 minutes per side until the internal temperature reaches 165 degrees Fahrenheit. Grill the vegetables alongside for 4-5 minutes. Serve with brown rice or quinoa. This meal provides lean protein, fiber, and essential vitamins. It is perfect for meal prepping and reheats well.

## Banana Smoothie Bowl
Blend two frozen bananas with half a cup of almond milk until thick and creamy. Pour into a bowl and top with granola, sliced strawberries, blueberries, coconut flakes, and a tablespoon of peanut butter. The frozen bananas create an ice-cream-like texture without added sugar. This bowl provides potassium, vitamin C, healthy fats, and sustained energy. For extra protein, add a scoop of protein powder or Greek yogurt to the blend.

## Lentil Soup
Lentil soup is hearty and nutritious. Saute one diced onion, two carrots, and two celery stalks in olive oil for 5 minutes. Add three minced garlic cloves, one teaspoon each of cumin and turmeric, and cook for another minute. Add one cup of red lentils, four cups of vegetable broth, and one can of diced tomatoes. Simmer for 25 minutes until lentils are tender. Season with salt, pepper, and lemon juice. Lentils are an excellent source of plant protein, iron, and folate.
